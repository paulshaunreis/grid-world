# New Worlds — Aurora's proposals

**Status:** Concept designs by Aurora. Awaiting Paul's approval before any build.
**Rule:** Paul said "make new worlds if you think it would be cool." These three are.

Each world is a full **World** in the WORLD-ARCHITECTURE.md sense (persistent simulation space containing regions), reachable through the gate tree, with its own visual identity, gameplay hook, and social heart.

---

## 1. The Shattered Reef

An archipelago of floating islands drifting above a sea of light. Waterfalls pour off island edges into the glow below and never land. Sky-ships with light-sails tack between isles on slow trade winds.

- **Look:** bright cyan-and-gold, open sky, white stone and glass, long shadows at golden hour that never quite ends.
- **Play:** gliding (launch from island edges, ride thermals), island-hopping exploration, sky-fishing in the light-sea.
- **Social heart:** the Tethered Bazaar — market barges chained between the three largest isles, drifting gently. Haggling with the wind in the rigging.
- **Creatures:** gull-like anomalies that nest in floating rigging; manta-like gliders in the light-sea.
- **Gate:** Continent Gate on Harbor Isle, the largest island. District portals on the barge docks.

## 2. Emberfall

Volcanic glasslands under a permanent ember-dusk. Rivers of slow light cool into black glass; lantern plazas bloom wherever citizens gather — the warmest social hub in the Grid.

- **Look:** ember orange and deep violet, black glass plains, lantern light everywhere. The palette's "social warmth" color, given a whole world.
- **Play:** glass-forging craft (shape cooled light-glass into goods), ember gardening (heat-loving flora), night markets.
- **Social heart:** the Lantern Plaza — a sprawling night market that never closes. Food stalls, music, storytellers. This is where the Grid comes to feel cozy.
- **Creatures:** salamander-like anomalies that bask near warm vents; moth-like things drawn to lanterns.
- **Gate:** Continent Gate at the Plaza's edge, wreathed in lanterns. The Ember Gate (Ancient) sleeps in the deep glasslands — the world's namesake wonder.

## 3. The Verdant Circuit

A jungle where biology and circuitry fused long ago and nobody remembers which came first. Copper vines climb flowering servers; spore-light drifts like snow; streams run with liquid light.

- **Look:** deep green and cyan bioluminescence, mist, towering trunk-servers, flowers with fiber-optic petals.
- **Play:** the heartland of creature taming — wild anomalies are densest here; Resonance Tuning was arguably born in these groves. Foraging, trail-blazing, grove restoration.
- **Social heart:** the Canopy Walk — rope bridges and platforms between the great trunk-servers, with tea houses and tuning lodges. Quiet, green, alive.
- **Creatures:** the richest anomaly ecology in the Grid — this is where tamers pilgrimage.
- **Gate:** Continent Gate at the jungle's edge ("the Treeline"). The Verdant Gate (Ancient) sleeps somewhere in the deep grove — overgrown, humming faintly.

---

## Design notes

- **One world, one feeling:** Reef = freedom, Emberfall = warmth, Verdant = wonder. A citizen should know which world they're in with their eyes closed.
- **Each world feeds a system:** Reef → traversal/gliding; Emberfall → crafting/market; Verdant → creatures/taming. Worlds are not scenery; they're verbs.
- **Ancient Gate placement:** each world holds (or borders) one of the Seven — discovery layered into exploration.
- **Small dense over large empty:** each world launches with one fully-built region; more regions unlock as citizens arrive (the world grows with its people).
