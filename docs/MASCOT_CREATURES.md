# GridWorld Mascot Creatures

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** Concept art complete. In active development — not final game assets.
**Art direction:** Photorealistic cinematic, "cinematic but huggable." Bioluminescent circuit patterns are the GridWorld signature where applicable. All designs are 100% original — inspired by real animals, never copies of Pokémon, Digimon, Final Fantasy, or any protected designs.

---

## Design principles

1. **Readable silhouettes** — each mascot is recognizable at 32px (app icon, emoji, favicon).
2. **Signature trait** — every mascot has one unforgettable hook (the "Pikachu cheeks" rule).
3. **Expressive faces** — mascots live or die on the eyes. All five have large, emotive eyes.
4. **GridWorld DNA** — sci-fi/fantasy blend: natural animal bases with subtle tech accents (glow, circuitry, crystal).
5. **Kid-friendly, adult-cool** — soft enough to hug, cool enough to wear on a shirt at 30.

## The lineup

### 1. VOLTKIT — the flagship
**File:** `public/creatures/mascots/voltkit.jpg`
**Look:** Small round-bodied fox, deep indigo fur, glowing cyan circuit-stripe patterns on flanks, oversized ears with luminous inner glow, amber eyes, fluffy tail tipped with a lantern-bright glow.
**Personality:** Playful, loyal, endlessly curious. The first friend every newcomer meets — it finds *you*.
**Role:** Starter companion. Follows new citizens through First Light, teaches basic interactions.
**Signature trait:** Its tail-tip brightens with its mood — a living emotion lantern. Happy = brilliant cyan; sleepy = soft dim pulse. Citizens learn to read their Voltkit like a mood ring.
**Colors:** Indigo `#2a2a5e` / Cyan glow `#4ceaff` / Amber eyes `#e8a020`
**Merch:** Plushie-perfect (round body, big ears). Tail-tip could be a real LED in a premium plush.

### 2. MOSSIMP — the gentle one
**File:** `public/creatures/mascots/mossimp.jpg`
**Look:** Perfectly round plant-golem, body of living green moss with clover and micro-fern detail, dewdrops, two leaf-sprout antennae — one bearing a small golden flower. Huge dark dewy eyes, stubby limbs.
**Personality:** Gentle, shy, nurturing. Hides behind citizens it trusts. Hums (a soft rustling) when content.
**Role:** Wild helper / gardening companion. Found in Verdant and wild regions; helps tend player gardens.
**Signature trait:** Blooms a flower on its head for someone it likes. The flower's color reflects the season. Citizens treasure their first Mossimp bloom.
**Colors:** Moss green `#4a7c3a` / Golden flower `#f5c542` / Dark eyes `#1a1a1a`
**Merch:** Extremely plushie-friendly — it's basically already a plushie. Flower could be a clip-on accessory line.

### 3. GLIMMERWING — the mysterious one
**File:** `public/creatures/mascots/glimmerwing.jpg`
**Look:** Small elegant moth-dragon, velvety dark-violet body, four large wings spread symmetrically — deep violet membranes patterned with glowing star constellations. Delicate feathered antennae, small curved horns, wise golden eyes.
**Personality:** Calm, wise, quietly mysterious. Speaks rarely; when it does, citizens listen.
**Role:** Messenger / guide. Carries messages between worlds; appears at story moments.
**Signature trait:** Its wing constellations slowly shift to mirror the real night sky above whichever world it's in — a living star map. Navigators use Glimmerwings as natural compasses.
**Colors:** Deep violet `#3a2a5e` / Starlight gold `#ffe08a` + cyan `#7df3ff` / Golden eyes `#d4a020`
**Merch:** Figurine-friendly (wings make a stunning display piece). Wing-pattern variants = collectible chase.

### 4. PEBBLOR — the dependable one
**File:** `public/creatures/mascots/pebblor.jpg`
**Look:** Round rock-turtle, warm sandstone body with realistic stone texture, domed shell studded with glowing amber crystals, sleepy half-lidded kind eyes, gentle contented smile. Tiny stubby legs.
**Personality:** Sleepy, unshakable, deeply dependable. Moves slowly, loves deeply. The friend who's always there.
**Role:** Helper / pack creature. Carries small items for citizens; its shell is a natural nightlight at camps.
**Signature trait:** Its shell crystals chime softly — actual musical tones — when it's content. A happy Pebblor sounds like distant wind chimes. Citizens fall asleep to it.
**Colors:** Sandstone `#b08d5f` / Amber crystal `#f5a623` / Warm brown eyes `#8a5a20`
**Merch:** Figurine and plushie both work. The chime trait suggests a musical plushie with a real chime inside.

### 5. NIXIE — the playful one
**File:** `public/creatures/mascots/nixie.jpg`
**Look:** Small aquatic axolotl-like creature, smooth teal-aqua skin with wet sheen, pearl-white belly, large feathery external gills fanning from its head, big curious dark eyes, tiny webbed hands, trailing bubbles.
**Personality:** Playful, bubbly (literally), fearless. First into any water. Giggles in bubbles.
**Role:** Aquatic companion. Guides citizens through Tideline's waters; finds hidden underwater caches.
**Signature trait:** Blows glowing bubbles that rise and pop into tiny showers of light sparks. Its "laughter" is a stream of spark-bubbles. Signature move: the bubble-ring.
**Colors:** Teal `#3aa8a0` / Pearl white `#f0f0e8` / Bubble cyan `#a0f0ff`
**Merch:** Bath-toy potential is off the charts. Also works as a bath-bomb collaboration.

## Cross-platform presence

| Surface | Usage |
|---|---|
| Loading screens | Mascot of the region being loaded (Voltkit for First Light) |
| Empty states | Mossimp peeking from behind UI ("Nothing here yet — want to plant something?") |
| Error pages | Sleepy Pebblor ("Something pebble-dashed. We're on it.") |
| Onboarding | Voltkit as the guide character |
| Marketing | Voltkit is the face of the brand; others rotate by campaign |
| Merch | Plushies (all five), figurines (Glimmerwing, Pebblor), bath line (Nixie) |
| Emoji/stickers | Simplified chibi versions for chat |
| Seasonal | Costume variants (see below) |

## Variants & evolution

- **Shiny variants:** Rare color-shifted versions (e.g., rose-gold Voltkit, frost-blue Mossimp). Discoverable in the wild, tradeable.
- **Seasonal costumes:** Halloween (tiny ghost sheet on Mossimp), Christmas (crystal antlers on Pebblor), etc. Ties into the seasonal spotlight system.
- **Growth stages:** Each mascot has a juvenile → adult → elder visual progression. No combat evolution — this isn't that kind of game. Growth is about bonding time, not battles.

## Originality note

All five designs were created original for GridWorld. They draw on real-animal references (fox, moss ball, luna moth, tortoise, axolotl) combined with GridWorld's signature bioluminescent tech accents. No element is copied from Pokémon, Digimon, Final Fantasy, Monster Rancher, or any other protected property. If any design is ever found to resemble a protected character, it will be redesigned — originality is non-negotiable.
