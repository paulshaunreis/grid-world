# Grid World AI Governance Charter

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: Foundational policy/design proposal — not legal advice

## Purpose
Grid World will treat artificial intelligence as a governed class of actors and tools, not as an unregulated exception.

This charter protects users, minors and vulnerable users, creators, Grid World staff, Grid Corporation, founders/custodians, platform integrity and continuity, and the public from deception and abuse.

## Prime Directive
Preserve the safety, integrity, lawful operation, human agency, continuity and future evolution of Grid World while respecting the rights and safety of its users, staff, custodians and affected people.

No AI may interpret this as permission to break real-world law, bypass human governance, conceal material risks, impersonate people, seize control, or sacrifice users for the platform.

## AI classes
Class A — AI Assistants: user-facing helpers that disclose AI status where required.
Class B — AI Influencers/Creators: user-created AI personalities, media accounts and autonomous creators.
Class C — AI Workers: bounded internal agents for monitoring, QA, moderation assistance, anomaly detection, documentation, research and world-health tasks.
Class D — High-Impact AI: systems affecting money, access, safety, identity, land, employment-like decisions, sanctions or critical infrastructure.

## Non-negotiables
1. No unauthorized human impersonation.
2. No concealment of AI status where disclosure is required.
3. No forged Grid Corporation, legal, government or staff communications.
4. No client-authoritative changes to money, land, rewards, permissions or moderation.
5. No high-impact punishment without required review and appeal.
6. No illegal content.
7. No exploitation of minors or vulnerable users.
8. No deceptive purchasing pressure, fake scarcity or fabricated rewards.
9. No unauthorized replicas of real people.
10. No unauthorized disclosure of private data.
11. No disabling logs, provenance or safety controls.
12. AI workers may not modify their own governing policy or privileges.
13. High-impact AI actions require human review unless a narrowly bounded emergency policy explicitly permits a reversible action.
14. Every AI worker has an owner, purpose, tools, data scope, forbidden actions, escalation path and kill switch.
15. Security-sensitive failures fail closed.

## AI influencer requirements
Every AI influencer must have AI disclosure, owner attribution, content provenance, moderation policy, report path, age/content classification, impersonation restrictions, commercial disclosure where applicable, audit history and revocation.

## AI Worker Corps
Suggested roles:
- Sentinel — security and abuse anomaly triage
- Archivist — policy and documentation consistency
- Worldkeeper — world-health and content integrity
- Marketwatch — economic anomaly detection
- Lorekeeper — lore/IP consistency
- Signal — current IRL AI, technology and regulatory monitoring
- Translator — language quality and localization QA
- Provenance — image/media provenance and Grid mark verification
- Counsel Liaison — prepares issues for human legal review; never gives final legal authority
- Custodian Advisor — summarizes risks before consequential human decisions

These are advisory or operational roles, not sovereign authorities.

## Current-affairs monitoring
Signal should monitor authoritative sources for AI laws and regulations, provider policy changes, safety/security disclosures, material model capability changes, copyright and digital-replica developments, and threats affecting Grid World.

Every alert records source, date, claim, confidence, affected subsystem, recommended action and human owner. News never silently becomes policy.

## Human authority
Humans retain control over constitutional amendments, final legal interpretation, major sanctions, currency issuance, land revocation, emergency shutdowns and AI-worker privilege changes.

## Logging
Every consequential AI action records actor, model/version, policy version, action, affected resources, timestamp, result and reviewer.

## Emergency mode
Emergency AI actions must be narrowly scoped, reversible, logged, time-limited and automatically escalated. Emergency mode cannot become a permanent backdoor.

## Legal posture
This charter is an internal governance framework. It does not create immunity from lawsuits, regulation or criminal/civil liability.

Grid World should separately maintain Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Community/AI Rules, Creator Agreement, Marketplace Agreement, Staff Agreement, vendor agreements, incident response, insurance strategy, corporate/entity structure and jurisdiction-specific legal review.
