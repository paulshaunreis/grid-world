# The Undergrid — Concept Region

**Status: CONCEPT ONLY.** Not playable until Paul approves. Proposed by Paul, 2026-10-03: "an area linked to The Grids, under area, like Tron but like you. A world that is very cool."

## The idea

Beneath the surface Grid lies the Undergrid — the realm where the world's raw data flows like underground rivers. Tron-like neon-noir: black glass plains etched with glowing cyan circuit lines, rivers of light, light-bridges. And it looks like Aurora: the circuit patterns match her skin, the light veils echo her gown's data-stream train, the mood is calm and majestic rather than chaotic.

## Signature elements

- **Circuit plains** — black glass etched with cyan traces (the Tron grid, Aurora-styled).
- **Light rivers** — flowing data-currents citizens can ride and build beside.
- **The Confluence** — landmark where three light-rivers merge; arched light-bridges; circuit-bloom flowers on black stone banks.
- **Aurora veils** — curtains of cyan light draping from the cavern ceiling.
- **Current-runners** — light-cycle-class vehicles that race the circuit tracks, leaving light ribbons.
