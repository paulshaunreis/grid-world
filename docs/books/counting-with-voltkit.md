# Counting with Voltkit

**A GridWorld picture book for ages 2–5**
**Status:** Manuscript — in active development

---

**Page 1 (Cover)**
*Illustration: Voltkit — a small round-bodied indigo fox with glowing cyan circuit-stripe patterns, oversized ears with luminous inner glow, amber eyes, and a fluffy tail with a lantern-bright glowing tip — bounding happily through First Light's glowing plaza at dawn. Numbers 1–10 float playfully in the sky.*

**Text:** Counting with Voltkit

---

**Page 2**
*Illustration: One Voltkit sitting in a sunlit meadow, tail-tip glowing bright cyan, smiling at the reader.*

**Text:** One little Voltkit, bright as can be. It found YOU — now count with me!

---

**Page 3**
*Illustration: Two round green Mossimps in a garden, one with a small golden flower blooming on its head, dewdrops sparkling on the moss.*

**Text:** Two soft Mossimps in the garden green. Count them with me: one… two!

---

**Page 4**
*Illustration: Three Nixies splashing in a turquoise lagoon, blowing glowing bubbles that pop into light sparks.*

**Text:** Three Nixies playing in the blue. Bubble, bubble, pop — count with me: one… two… three!

---

**Page 5**
*Illustration: Four Pebblors — round rock-turtles with glowing amber crystal shells — plodding across warm sandstone at sunset. One's crystals are chiming.*

**Text:** Four Pebblors walking slow and steady. One… two… three… four!

---

**Page 6**
*Illustration: Five Glimmerwings — elegant violet moth-dragons with star-constellation wings — gliding across a twilight sky full of real stars.*

**Text:** Five Glimmerwings flying through the night. Their wings hold the stars — count them: one… two… three… four… five!

---

**Page 7**
*Illustration: Six Mossimps having a picnic in a clover field, sharing berries, flowers blooming on several heads.*

**Text:** Six Mossimps sharing berries sweet. One… two… three… four… five… six!

---

**Page 8**
*Illustration: Seven Voltkit kits (baby Voltkits, smaller with dimmer tail-tips) tumbling and playing in a pile, laughing.*

**Text:** Seven baby Voltkits in a tumbling heap! One… two… three… four… five… six… seven!

---

**Page 9**
*Illustration: Eight Nixies in a circle blowing one giant glowing bubble-ring together.*

**Text:** Eight Nixies blowing bubbles high. One… two… three… four… five… six… seven… eight!

**Page 10**
*Illustration: Nine Pebblors asleep in a cozy pile, shells glowing softly like nightlights, crystals chiming a lullaby.*

**Text:** Nine sleepy Pebblors, glowing soft and deep. One… two… three… four… five… six… seven… eight… nine… shhh!

---

**Page 11**
*Illustration: Ten — one grand celebration scene! All five mascots together in First Light's plaza: Voltkit, Mossimp, Glimmerwing, Pebblor, Nixie, plus five more friends, confetti of light-sparks in the air.*

**Text:** Ten friends together — hooray, hooray! One… two… three… four… five… six… seven… eight… nine… TEN!

**Page 12 (Back cover)**
*Illustration: Voltkit curled up asleep, tail-tip glowing a soft dim pulse like a nightlight.*

**Text:** You counted all the way to ten! Voltkit is proud of you. Sweet dreams, little counter.
