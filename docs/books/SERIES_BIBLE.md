# GridWorld Book Series — Series Bible

**Date:** 2026-10-08
**Status:** In active development — manuscripts and sample art in progress
**Owner:** Paul Shaun Reis, Creative Director

This is the master document for every book in the GridWorld library. One cohesive world, one voice, one visual identity — from a toddler's first numbers to a teen's first webpage.

---

## Core principles (all books)

1. **Mascots as guides, not lecturers.** Voltkit, Mossimp, Glimmerwing, Pebblor, and Nixie walk alongside the reader. They wonder, they try, they make mistakes, they learn.
2. **Real knowledge, honestly simplified.** Never dumbed down to the point of being wrong. A 5-year-old's explanation of a black hole must still be true.
3. **Pictures on every spread.** Visual learners first — diagrams, illustrations, visual metaphors throughout.
4. **Wonder energy.** Paul's north stars: Stephen Hawking's and Neil deGrasse Tyson's awe at the universe. Every book should make the reader feel the world is bigger and more beautiful than they knew.
5. **Honesty about the world.** GridWorld books never promise what's not real. (The economy book is upfront: GWC is fictional, no cash-out yet.)
6. **© GridWorld** on every book. All art, characters, and text are Paul Shaun Reis's property.

### Core subjects track (the curriculum spine)

**Math:**
- **Mossimp's Math** (5–8) — addition, subtraction, multiplication, visual and friendly. Counting berries with Mossimp, grouping Nixie's bubbles.
- **Pebblor's Numbers** (9–12) — fractions, decimals, early algebra. Slow, careful, worked examples. Pebblor never rushes; neither does the reader.

**English:**
- **Nixie's Words** (5–8) — vocabulary, reading comprehension, storytelling. GridWorld lore as reading material.
- **Glimmerwing's Grammar** (9–12) — grammar, sentence structure, writing clearly. Precise like star maps.
- **Aurora's Stories** (9–14) — creative writing, how stories work. Writing prompts featuring the mascots. Ends with the reader writing their own GridWorld tale.

Together with science + coding, this is a full curriculum spine — a kid could learn real school subjects through GridWorld.

### Creator tracks

- **Build with Voltkit: HTML & CSS for Beginners** (9–14)
  Voltkit as guide (circuits → code). Zero assumed knowledge. Every chapter builds something real and visible in a browser. GridWorld-themed projects: a mascot profile page, a styled landing page. Ends with their own customized page. Mistakes are part of learning — honest, encouraging tone.
  **Art:** `public/books/build-with-voltkit-cover-color.webp`

- **How To Make Money on GridWorld** (teens and up)
  The practical guide to earning in the GridWorld economy: GWC, marketplace selling, the Wanderer/Citizen/Architect tiers, selling creations, building for others, running a shop, creating content. Honest about what's real now vs. coming (GWC is fictional, no cash-out yet — stated upfront). No get-rich-quick promises; real work, real skill. Paul's sincerity throughout — he knows what it's like to be broke and wants this to be a genuine opportunity. When creator payouts go live, this book becomes the manual.
  **Art:** `public/books/make-money-on-gridworld-cover-color.webp`

## Format specs

Every title ships in two editions from day one:

### Color edition (premium)
- Full-color illustrations, rich cinematic palette
- **Digital:** EPUB (reflowable where possible; fixed-layout for picture books), Kindle (KF8), PDF
- **Print:** Print-on-demand paperback + hardcover; picture books 8.5"×8.5" or 10"×8"; Explains 7"×10"; Art book 12"×12" large format
- Recommended: 300 DPI source art, sRGB for digital, CMYK conversion for print

### Black-and-white edition
- **Not just desaturated** — purpose-built line art: thick confident ink lines, open shapes, no fine gradients, designed for e-ink from the first stroke
- **E-ink Kindle edition:** clean B&W, high contrast, no gray washes that muddy on e-ink
- **Coloring-book variant:** the B&W line art as a standalone coloring book — thicker lines, simpler shapes, perforated pages in print
- **Digital:** EPUB, Kindle, PDF (smaller file sizes than color)

### Shared specs
- All books: © GridWorld, Paul Shaun Reis. ISBN per edition.
- Accessibility: alt-text on all illustrations in digital editions, dyslexia-friendly font option in EPUB
- No DRM on direct-from-GridWorld sales; standard DRM where retailers require it

## How the lines tie together

- The **same five mascots** guide every line — a 3-year-old meeting Voltkit in the counting book meets the same Voltkit at 12 learning HTML.
- **Recurring visual language:** circuit patterns, star constellations, moss textures, crystal glows, bubbles — the mascots' signature traits become teaching tools (Voltkit's tail-tip = mood/feelings gauge AND electricity metaphor).
- **Graduation path:** Sprout → Explorer → Voyager → Navigator mirrors growing up with GridWorld. The books grow with the reader.
- **Worldbuilding consistency:** every fact about the mascots, places, and lore matches the game canon. The books ARE GridWorld, on paper.
