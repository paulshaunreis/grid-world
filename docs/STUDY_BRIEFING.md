## The 10 stealable ideas (mapped to our systems)

1. **Keyword-addressed zones (.hack):** three-word phrases generate explorable areas; the server/shard changes the result; good addresses become community currency; hidden addresses drop through events. → Our gate tree's "lock-in" could accept keyword plates for wild/unlisted destinations.
2. **Chaos Gate travel ritual (.hack):** warping as a deliberate, creative, social act — never a skipped loading screen. → Already our design; protect it.
3. **Simulated out-of-world layer (.hack G.U. desktop):** email, forums, news, customization that update with world events. → Our unified UI + GridWatch + site already point here; add an in-fiction "citizen desktop" with Historium news and Aura notices.
4. **Discovery beyond the interface (Log Horizon):** mastery = techniques the UI doesn't teach; emergent discoveries drive the economy. → Crafting/tuning should hide real techniques for players to discover and teach.
5. **Notice-board contract loops (Witcher 3):** post → investigate → prepare → resolve; the fight is the least interesting part. → Citizen-posted bounties/requests as repeatable social content.
6. **Tradeoff curses (Shangri-La Frontier):** voluntary risk/reward loadouts. → Anomaly-taming traits with real tradeoffs, player-chosen.
7. **Server-wide unique events (Shangri-La Frontier's Seven Colossi):** one-of-each, non-respawning, never RNG-gated, unknown criteria → rumor/wiki meta. → Our Ancient Gate awakenings should work like this.
8. **Player-authored NPCs (Overlord):** NPCs with written personalities as player expression. → Citizen-authored helper NPCs for plots/shops.
9. **Guild-as-legal-entity (SAO):** Contract Scroll — treasury, roster, delegable permissions. → Citizen groups with real treasuries and roles.
10. **Civic infrastructure as ownable/governable (Log Horizon Round Table):** endgame = giving power back. → City governance tools; districts citizens can actually run.

## Warnings (what killed the others)

- **.hack died** from four full-price discs with no iteration, mystery-box writing that never paid off, and Bandai Namco abandoning the West — then SAO out-published it. Lesson: ship iteratively, pay off mysteries, never stop publishing.
- **Rec Room died June 2026 with 150M users** — costs beat revenue. Lesson: 150M users ≠ a business; measure concurrency-per-space and creator earnings, not registrations.
- **Meta burned ~$80B on Horizon Worlds** by gating social behind VR hardware. Lesson: browser-first is the right call; never gate behind hardware.
- **Decentraland/Sandbox land crashed ~95%** — artificial scarcity without activity. Lesson: engagement economy before land economy; never sell scarcity before the world is alive.
- **OpenSim:** an open server without a viewer, content, and users is a ghost town; whoever controls the viewer controls the roadmap. Lesson: own our client.
- **Second Life survived 20+ years** on one decision: creators own their IP + real payouts. Lesson: bake creator ownership into the social contract from day one.

## The Aura separation (legal/creative safety)

.hack has its own "Aura" (ultimate AI born from player data, girl-form, sacrificial rebirth). Our Aura must stay clearly distinct — documented no-fly list:
- NOT born from player data; NOT a sacrificial-rebirth figure; NOT an aloof goddess in the .hack mold.
- OURS: System Administrator under Aurora, mysterious like .hack's *thematic role* but original in origin, visuals, and function. Pale, white-haired, nine-ish, whited-out zone, stops viruses on a whim, "Some doors open only for Aurora."
- Keep this separation in the worldbuilding bible permanently.

## What Oct 2026 trends mean for us

- **Co-op with friends dominates** (WARDOGS 3.2M copies; cozy fishing co-ops trending). Our lane: low-stakes co-op, not extraction stakes.
- **Asia + low-spec = reach** (Onimusha's #1 Steam country: China). Performance is a feature — the dynamic-resolution/FRS1 web plan is validated.
- **The word "metaverse" is dead; the desire isn't.** Never call it that. It's a world, a neighborhood, a clubhouse.
- **Live events are the retention engine.** The world needs a calendar, not just a map — Crack Days, gate raisings, seasonal shifts.

## Do / Don't (standing)

**Do:** browser-native; creators own IP + real payouts; free tools; curated onboarding with a default hangout; engagement before land; moderation-by-design from day one; own the client; graceful-exit plan (creator export tools).
**Don't:** gate on hardware/downloads; sell scarcity before activity; monetize before community; bolt on safety; ship empty; promise what the build can't do.
