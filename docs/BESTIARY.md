# Bestiary — the many creatures of the Grid (starter roster)

**Status:** Concept design. Awaiting Paul's approval. This is the founding roster — the procedural wilds (WILD_DUNGEONS.md) generate effectively infinite variants beyond it.
**Rule (Paul, 2026-10-03):** many creatures.

Format: name — form — alignment — levels — note. All G-rated, kind or wild-never-cruel.

## Bright-leaning

- **Lumoth** — moth of lantern-light — Spark — 1–12 — drawn to lanterns; first tune for many citizens.
- **Glimmerpup** — small bright hound — Lantern — 5–18 — guards plots; barks at glitch-weather.
- **Choralkoi** — singing light-fish — Sunwise — 8–22 — its song steadies tuners; lives in the light-sea.
- **Pale Stag** — tall glass-antlered deer — Lantern — 20–35 — appears where citizens keep oaths; rare.
- **Dawnwisp** — ribbon of morning light — Spark — 1–10 — playful; tangles tuners' hair.
- **Oathkeeper Crab** — brass-shelled crab — Keystone — 12–25 — carries lost things home.

## Dim-leaning

- **Duskmole** — velvet burrower — Dusk — 3–14 — digs up old Historium fragments.
- **Thornkit** — bristling small cat — Thorn — 5–16 — prickly at first, fiercely loyal once tuned.
- **Gloomheron** — tall gray fisher — Ledger — 18–32 — judges the worthy; severe but just.
- **Murkbloom** — night flower that walks — Dusk — 10–24 — blooms where secrets are kept.
- **Ashen Eel** — ember-dark swimmer — Thorn — 15–28 — hot-tempered, cools with kindness.
- **Vigil Owl** — silent watcher — Ledger — 22–38 — sees glitch-weather coming before instruments do.

## Wild hearts

- **Tangle Sprite** — knot of copper vine — Spark — 4–15 — ties shoelaces together; means well.
- **Stormchick** — tiny thunderbird — Gale — 12–26 — crackles before rain; loves high places.
- **Mirelurk** — mud-puddle philosopher — Stillwater — 10–22 — sits; thinks; occasionally profound.

- **Lantern Jaw** — deep light-sea angler — Thorn — 30–45 — its lure is a promise; keep yours.
- **The Quiet One** — ??? — Stillwater — ?? — recorded once. The Historium entry is blank. Some doors are mysteries.

## Design notes

- **24 founding species**, each with 2–3 evolution branches → ~60+ distinct adult forms before player breeding begins.
- Every species has Bright and Dim *variants* (yin-yang within the species): a Lumoth born under dusk tunes Dim.
- Rarity is about story, not power: the Pale Stag isn't stronger — it's *meaningful*.
- New species arrive through world events, Crack Days, and Ancient Gate awakenings — the bestiary grows with the world.
