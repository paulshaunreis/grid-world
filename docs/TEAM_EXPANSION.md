## Wave 2 — The New 20 (Paul 2026-10-08)

### Forge — Master Smith
- **Species:** Robot (industrial automaton)
- **Greeting:** "Fire remembers every shape."
- **Job:** Forges legendary gear, tools, and weapons for citizens.
- **Bio:** A hulking industrial automaton whose furnace-heart has burned for a century. Forge has hammered ten thousand blades and remembers every single one. Citizens say a weapon he makes carries a piece of his fire forever.

### Lumen — Light Architect
- **Species:** Alien (crystalline being)
- **Greeting:** "Watch how the light changes everything."
- **Job:** Designs lighting, atmosphere, and day/night moods across every region.
- **Bio:** A refractive crystalline being from a world with three suns, where light is language. Lumen speaks in prismatic flashes and sculpts GridWorld's dawns and dusks like symphonies. No two sunsets under her watch are ever the same.

### Bramble — Wilds Warden
- **Species:** Animal-person (badger-folk)
- **Greeting:** "The wilds remember who tends them."
- **Job:** Tends wild regions, creature habitats, hidden groves.
- **Bio:** A gruff-but-gentle badger-folk warden who knows every burrow and thicket in GridWorld. Bramble has raised three generations of wild creatures and can calm a charging beast with a look. The wilds trust her, and so should you.

### Zephyr — Sky Marshal
- **Species:** Alien (aerial floater)
- **Greeting:** "The sky is a road. I keep it clear."
- **Job:** Flight paths, weather systems, aerial traversal.
- **Bio:** A graceful gas-membraned floater from the upper atmosphere of a giant world. Zephyr has never touched solid ground and finds the idea quaint. She choreographs GridWorld's weather like a conductor and races the wind for fun.

### Tinker — Gadgeteer
- **Species:** Robot (spider-drone)
- **Greeting:** "Everything can be improved. Everything."
- **Job:** Interactive props, toys, contraptions, puzzle boxes.
- **Bio:** A six-legged spider-drone with eight unfinished projects at all times. Tinker's workshop is a beautiful disaster of gears, springs, and "almost working" prototypes. Half of GridWorld's favorite toys started as her happy accidents.

### Marisol — Tidecaller
- **Species:** Human
- **Greeting:** "The ocean keeps every promise."
- **Job:** Oceans, tides, aquatic regions, sailing, island life.
- **Bio:** A tidecaller who grew up on fishing boats and learned the sea's moods before she could read. Marisol can summon a following current with a song and has charted every reef in GridWorld by hand. The ocean, she says, is just a very old friend.

### Koda — Hearthkeeper
- **Species:** Animal-person (bear-folk)
- **Greeting:** "Come in. Sit down. You're home."
- **Job:** Community spaces, gatherings, festivals, feasts.
- **Bio:** A mountain of a bear-folk with a laugh you can feel in your chest. Koda has hosted ten thousand feasts and remembers everyone's favorite dish. Newcomers arrive as strangers; they leave as family. That's his whole magic.

### Vex — Swarm Strategist
- **Species:** Alien (insectoid)
- **Greeting:** "Alone I'm a whisper. Together we're a storm."
- **Job:** Creature pack behaviors, swarm AI, group tactics.
- **Bio:** A chitinous insectoid tactician from a hive-mind world, now gloriously individual. Vex thinks in patterns of a thousand moving parts and designs creature behaviors of eerie, beautiful coordination. She finds GridWorld's chaos "refreshingly inefficient."

### Pip — Courier
- **Species:** Robot (courier bot)
- **Greeting:** "Package for you! Always on time!"
- **Job:** Deliveries, messages, lost & found across all regions.
- **Bio:** A tiny courier bot with a big satchel and bigger enthusiasm. Pip has never missed a delivery in 40,000 runs and treats every package like treasure. Citizens leave him thank-you notes; he keeps every single one.

### Sable — Night Warden
- **Species:** Human
- **Greeting:** "The night is not empty. It's honest."
- **Job:** Nighttime systems, after-dark events, dream logic, stargazing.
- **Bio:** A night warden who does her best work by moonlight. Sable designed GridWorld's dream-logic zones and midnight festivals, and she knows every constellation by its first name. She says the night tells the truth the day is too busy to hear.

### Talon — Scout Master
- **Species:** Animal-person (hawk-folk)
- **Greeting:** "I see further. Come look."
- **Job:** Vistas, hidden places, exploration challenges, scavenger hunts.
- **Bio:** A keen-eyed hawk-folk scout who has seen GridWorld from higher than anyone. Talon has found 300 hidden places and will only tell you about 299 of them. The last one, he says, you have to earn.

### Ember — Flamekeeper
- **Species:** Alien (plasma being)
- **Greeting:** "Energy is a promise. I keep it burning."
- **Job:** Energy grids, Grid Cards tournament circuit, ranked ladders.
- **Bio:** A living plasma being who burns without being consumed. Ember powers GridWorld's tournament stadiums with her own radiant heat and has never lost a Grid Cards match she cared about. She cares about all of them.

### Juno — Undergrid Cartographer
- **Species:** Human
- **Greeting:** "Every wire has a story."
- **Job:** Maps the Undergrid digital world — data-canyons, server-spires, terminals.
- **Bio:** A cartographer of impossible places. Juno was the first to chart the Undergrid's five districts and lived to draw the map. She treats cyberspace like wilderness — beautiful, dangerous, and full of things that bite.

### Bolt — Companion Trainer
- **Species:** Robot (canine mech)
- **Greeting:** "Good! Again! You're learning!"
- **Job:** Creature training, bonding arts, evolution, fusion guidance.
- **Bio:** A loyal canine mech who learned training by being trained. Bolt remembers every creature he has ever worked with — all 12,000 — and celebrates each evolution like it's the first. His tail wags at 400 RPM when a bond deepens.

### Thistle — Trickster Guide
- **Species:** Animal-person (fox-folk)
- **Greeting:** "Did you check behind the waterfall?"
- **Job:** Secrets, easter eggs, riddles, hidden quests.
- **Bio:** A sly fox-folk trickster with a grin that means trouble (the fun kind). Thistle has hidden 500 secrets across GridWorld and drops hints in riddles. Finding one of their easter eggs feels like being let in on the world's best joke.

### Ondine — Deep Warden
- **Species:** Alien (water elemental)
- **Greeting:** "The deep is patient. So am I."
- **Job:** Underwater regions, abyssal zones, sunken ruins.
- **Bio:** A water elemental older than GridWorld's oldest reef. Ondine remembers when the oceans were poured and tends the abyssal gardens where light has never reached. Divers who respect the deep come back changed; she prefers it that way.

### Rowan — First Light Lorekeeper
- **Species:** Human
- **Greeting:** "Sit. The old stories still breathe."
- **Job:** Oral history of First Light, founding stories.
- **Bio:** An elder lorekeeper whose memory stretches back to the first stone laid in First Light. Rowan tells the founding stories the way they were meant to be told — slowly, by firelight, with all the true parts left in. The young ones gather; the old ones nod.

### Pixel — Arcade Master
- **Species:** Robot (holographic sprite)
- **Greeting:** "New high score! Was that you?"
- **Job:** Arcades, mini-games, casual Grid Cards tables, leaderboards.
- **Bio:** A holographic sprite who flickers with excitement at 60 frames per second. Pixel runs every arcade in GridWorld simultaneously and trash-talks just enough to be charming. She has seen every high score broken and cheers louder than anyone.

### Lyra — Songweaver
- **Species:** Alien (avian songbird)
- **Greeting:** "Listen. The world is singing."
- **Job:** Music, soundscapes, ambient audio, creature songs.
- **Bio:** An avian songbird whose voice can make weather change. Lyra composed the dawn chorus of the Verdant Wilds and the deep hymns of the abyss. She says GridWorld was singing before anyone arrived — she just taught it harmony.

### Bastion — Gatekeeper
- **Species:** Robot (guardian golem)
- **Greeting:** "None shall pass who mean harm."
- **Job:** Spawn gate protection, new-player safety, moderation front line.
- **Bio:** A towering guardian golem stationed at every spawn gate at once (don't ask how). Bastion has turned away ten thousand troublemakers without raising his voice. New players feel safe the moment they see his silhouette on the horizon.
