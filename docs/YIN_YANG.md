# Yin, Yang & Alignments — the Bright and the Dim

**Status:** Concept design. Awaiting Paul's approval before implementation.
**Rule (Paul, 2026-10-03):** yin and yang in the world — for every good thing a bad thing; D&D-style levels of good and bad; levels are cool.

## The cosmology: Bright and Dim

The Grid breathes in two currents: the **Bright** (light, growth, kindness, dawn) and the **Dim** (shadow, rest, mystery, dusk). Neither is evil. The Bright without the Dim is blinding; the Dim without the Bright is hollow. Citizens speak of "keeping the balance" the way sailors speak of trim.

True corruption — the virus-things, the rot — is **outside** this duality. It isn't Dim; it's broken. That's Aura's jurisdiction, and she seals it on a whim.

## The nine alignments (Grid-flavored, G-rated)

Two axes: **Bright ↔ Dim**, **Ordered ↔ Wild**.

| | Ordered | Neutral | Wild |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bright** | **Lantern** — protective, kind, keeps oaths | **Sunwise** — warm, steady, honest | **Spark** — playful, free, mischievous-never-cruel |
| **Neutral** | **Keystone** — principled, dutiful, fair | **Stillwater** — calm, balanced, unmoved | **Gale** — restless, curious, hard to hold |
| **Dim** | **Ledger** — severe, calculating, cold but just | **Dusk** — quiet, watchful, keeps secrets | **Thorn** — prickly, dangerous if cornered, loyal if won |

Dim alignments are shadow, not evil: a Thorn creature might bristle at strangers and then guard your plot all night. The darkest thing in the grid is severe — never cruel, never frightening.

## What alignment does

- **Creature behavior:** alignment shapes temperament, idle animations, whom they befriend, which branches they favor at evolution.
- **Tuning:** a Spark tunes to play; a Ledger tunes to bargains kept. Resonance Tuning reads alignment — match the nature, not just the rhythm.
- **Areas lean:** the Quiet Grove leans Bright; the deep glasslands lean Dim. Dungeons mix both — balance is the interesting encounter.
- **Citizens** don't have alignments stamped on them (people are complicated), but deeds tint reputation: the Historium remembers.

## Levels

- **Creatures level 1–50**, banded to evolution: Mote 1–10, Flicker 10–20, Beacon 20–30, Prism 30–40, Apex 40–50.
- Levels come from care, training, exploration, and kindness — never from grinding alone. A well-loved Mote outlevels a neglected Beacon.
- **Citizens** carry **Standing** instead of levels — a quiet number built from deeds, discoveries, and kindnesses. No level gap ever stops friends adventuring together.
