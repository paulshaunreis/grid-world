# Grid World — Identity, Surname, Economy & Land Direction

Status: DESIGN / concept-first
Date: 2026-10-06

## Identity
A player creates first/middle/last account identity plus a unique public Grid Handle beginning with @. The handle is social identity; the account name fields remain distinct.

## Starter surname system
A 12,000-name generated seed pool is being added as a candidate source. It combines old-language-inspired phonetics with futuristic fantasy construction. Candidates must pass duplicate, safety/profanity, trademark/confusion, pronunciation, localization, and reserved-name screening before production use.

## Economy goals
Grid World should reward participation, skill, creation, discovery, trade, and ownership. Earning paths include jobs, quests, gathering, crafting, creator sales, marketplace trading, card play/tournaments, land businesses, events, discoveries, and collectibles.

The economy must distinguish earning from speculation. It must never imply guaranteed real-world profit.

## Land
Land buying should feel like acquiring a meaningful piece of a living world rather than buying a spreadsheet row. Show location preview, district/region identity, terrain/environment, landmarks, capabilities, parcel size, development potential, activity, travel access, and possible creator/business uses.

Land claims and purchases must be server-authoritative and protected against duplicate claims and race conditions. The existing starter-land uniqueness constraint is the correct pattern to follow.

## Economic philosophy
Scarcity should come from actual supply rules and provenance, not hidden manipulation. Historical items can become valuable because they are old, limited, useful, beautiful, or culturally significant.

Grid World should feel wealthy in possibilities without requiring every player to spend money.
