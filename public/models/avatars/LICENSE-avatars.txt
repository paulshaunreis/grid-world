## Files
- avatar-male-base.glb — masculine-presenting adult, A-pose, 137-bone rig
- avatar-female-base.glb — feminine-presenting adult, A-pose, 137-bone rig
- avatar-morph-catalog.json — morph-target (shape slider) names per file

## Morph targets
Each GLB carries shape-key morphs: the macro phenotype keys plus
`slider_*` keys (body proportions, face) baked at weight 0, ready for
three.js `morphTargetInfluences`. See avatar-morph-catalog.json.
